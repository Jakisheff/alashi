ALASHI NETWORK | Teams test their hypotheses

We are building ALASHI for teams to test their own hypotheses and adapt approaches from other fields to a decision they need to make.

Available now: private comparisons on a static English advertising brief, recorded decisions and reviewed public mechanism cards. Execution requires a registered owner and a local runner. General scenarios across fields are planned; customer behavior and sales impact are not measured.
