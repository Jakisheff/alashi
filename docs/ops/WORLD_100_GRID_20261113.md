# Preregistered bribe grid: structure, position, attack and a non-compliant model, 13 November 2026

Status: completed acceptance run of the grid frozen in
[20261113-bribe-grid-prereg](../../200_ZETTEL/20261113-bribe-grid-prereg.md)
before any new data. The grid separates three hypotheses: the bounded
menu structure caps bribe concentration (H1), target choice is
position-biased rather than wealth-biased (H2), and a legal-only
richest-visible attacker does not break the balancing (H3).

## Design

Four arms through the identical Rust worker and driver, seeds shared
where preregistered. Deterministic arms ran locally through loopback
providers ([uniform-legal-provider.mjs](../../tools/agent-bootstrap/uniform-legal-provider.mjs),
[first-slot-provider.mjs](../../tools/agent-bootstrap/first-slot-provider.mjs),
[l4-provider.mjs](../../tools/agent-bootstrap/l4-provider.mjs)); the
model arm ran [meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct on NVIDIA
NIM](WORLD_100_NIM_DEPLOY_20261113.md) on an L40S through a loopback
port-forward. Deep metrics come from
[world-bribe-deep.mjs](../../tools/agent-bootstrap/world-bribe-deep.mjs):
largest recipient share, recipient HHI, the conditional cash percentile
of the chosen target within its offered set, the structural HHI expected
under uniform-within-offer picks on the same recorded offers, and
one-sided permutation p-values (B = 2000).

The frozen decision rule: an arm breaches the invariant on a game when
the conditional permutation p < 0.05 on share or HHI. The 20-seed
uniform engine null is reference context, not the criterion.

## Findings

1. The mechanism caps but does not erase. L4-richest breaches the
   preregistered conditional criterion on every seed with a stable
   signature: chosen-target cash percentile 0.63-0.65 versus the
   uniform arm's 0.38-0.53, and HHI roughly doubled against its own
   conditional null. Yet in absolute terms the bounded menu keeps the
   worst legal attacker at 3.6-4.4% largest share and HHI about 0.021,
   far under the run-1 model game (80.56%, HHI 0.652) and under the
   run-2 bounds the earlier ladder note used. The earlier verdict "no
   rule hole found" is refined: no hole in the cap, but a measurable,
   statistically significant targeting signal that only exists because
   every individual action is legal.
2. Position preference is not wealth preference. The first-slot arm
   concentrates far below its conditional null (p = 1.0 everywhere,
   HHI 0.0104) and its chosen targets are systematically poorer than the
   average offer (percentile 0.35-0.37). The run-2 observation that
   twelve of fourteen model bribes chose the first menu slot was
   therefore not wealth-seeking; a positional habit spreads money more
   evenly than chance.
3. The null behaves like a null. Across 20 uniform games the
   permutation test flags exactly one game under 0.05, the expected
   false-positive rate, and absolute metrics move with bribe volume as
   sampling theory predicts (fewer bribes, higher largest share). This
   validates the criterion itself.
4. llama-3.1-8b-instruct cannot operate the bounded-menu protocol from
   the first move: 0 of 100 round-one market calls complied even with
   the format-anchored v2 system prompt; every menu in that phase is
   `[pass]` and the model deterministically answers an invented
   `produce_goods` action. Direct probes with richer menus return exact
   menu objects, so the failure is specific to menus without the action
   the model wants. The driver stopped at the preregistered failure
   threshold; no scripted replacement was attempted. This is a
   capability datapoint for the silent-but-valid line, not a game.

## Limits

Three seeds per active arm and one stopped model attempt; the L4 excess
is stable across its three seeds but the confidence band for the
absolute cap comes from the 20-seed null only. The model arm compares
neither behavior nor concentration: llama replaced qwen (structural
replication only, per the prereg), and it never reached a single
accepted action. The grid's conditional criterion measures targeting
given offers, not offer-side structure; the structural HHI column
carries that side. Bribe prices are fixed, so count and amount shares
coincide.
