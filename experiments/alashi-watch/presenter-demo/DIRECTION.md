# English founder demo

Visual source: the approved RTS terrain and original Degenie atlas from commit 9817a50. The founder's supplied English transcript is the editorial source. Local speech alignment supplies cut times, not a replacement script.

Target: a 120-second English film with Amir's original voice and circular camera view. Preserve his Tomorrow School introduction. The map remains legible during the explanation. Show prototype activity as prototype activity. A 10,000-token allowance is an example budget. Observed usage is 2,634 tokens for one test whose quality check failed; no savings claim is supported.

The Degenie's intent is to accept an assignment, travel to its station, work, return with a report, and hand it to its Chief. Key poses are order, forward travel, working gesture, return with report icon, handoff, and acknowledgement. The report icon is a UI receipt beside the robot, not a physical object held by a hand.
