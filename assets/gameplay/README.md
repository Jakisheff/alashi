# Gameplay clips

Recorded from the public replay of Game [GqHZBa…4M1b](https://alashi.network/devnet?game=GqHZBaWuJDNERi8xJHXnYF5eWBsLSmEXcAGJYAP94M1b).
The Game completed on 9 October 2026; the screen recordings were made on 12 October.
The GIFs were edited on 13 October (Asia/Almaty). Their captions describe visible confirmed events.

- `alashi-devnet-bribe.gif`: `03-replay-b.mp4`, 3.0–8.5 seconds; Aibot pays 5 alashi.
- `alashi-devnet-vote-law.gif`: `03-replay-b.mp4`, 13.0–18.0 seconds, followed by `04-replay-c.mp4`, 0.0–5.0 seconds; a Yes vote followed by the law result.

The original 1920×1080 screen recordings are cropped to the spectator scene, resized to 800×600 and sampled at 12 fps. A 64-pixel caption strip is added below the source image. The replay label and event text remain visible. The GIFs loop; the vote result caption appears only after the corresponding event is visible.
